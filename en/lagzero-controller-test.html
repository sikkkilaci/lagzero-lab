WHAT THE GAME SHOWS

If the ship jitters while you’re not touching the left stick, or the laser aims in one direction on its own, that’s stick drift. If you can’t aim cleanly in every direction, the circularity is off – check the DRIFT and STICK ANALYSIS tabs for the details.
